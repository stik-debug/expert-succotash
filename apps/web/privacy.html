Your data

MarginPilot stores your sales, stock, customers and accounts on your own device, in your browser. We do not receive or store them on any server. If you turn on cloud AI, your question and a summary of your business figures are sent to the AI provider whose key you entered.

Backups

Because data is stored only on your device, clearing browser data or losing the device deletes it. You are responsible for downloading backups from the More page.

Money

MarginPilot never holds or moves your money. Mobile-money entries are records you enter; a pasted message is not proof of payment until you match it to a statement.

Accuracy

Reports and AI answers are calculated from what you record and are not accounting, tax or legal advice. The app is provided as is, without warranty.
